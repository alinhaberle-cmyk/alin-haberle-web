About

I'm a writer from Brooklyn, New York.

I write speculative fiction and fantasy. My stories are about dreams, ghosts, and old friends. I also write poetry.

I have a BA in English with a concentration in Creative Writing from Fordham University. I'm currently in the English MA program at Hunter College.

FAQ

Nope! I don't use generative technology ("AI") at any stage in my writing process. I didn't use AI to create this website, either.

Some of my favorite authors are Diana Wynne Jones, Helen Oyeyemi, Kelly Link, Agatha Christie, Ray Bradbury, T. Kingfisher, and Virginia Woolf. My favorite book of all time is Howl's Moving Castle by Diana Wynne Jones. (I love the movie, too!)

I also love graphic novels: Paper Girls (Brian K. Vaughan, Cliff Chiang) and Hobtown Mystery Stories (Alexander Forbes, Kris Bertin) are two of my recent favorites.

I'm not on any social media, sorry! Please feel free to reach out using my contact page.
